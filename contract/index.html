1Parties

This agreement is between Shinafi Digital Services ("Provider"), phone 918-852-5680, email fm.mamah@gmail.com, and the dealership named below ("Client").

2Term and fee

This agreement runs from the effective date through March 31, 2027. Client pays $600 per month for each month of that term. The fee covers everything in Section 3. Paid ad budgets for Facebook or Instagram are not included and go directly from Client to the platform. The agreement ends on March 31, 2027 unless both parties renew in writing, and rates may change at renewal.

3Scope of services

  • APhotography. Provider photographs vehicles and the lot as inventory is received and sold. Client tells Provider about new arrivals and sales, and sessions are scheduled around that activity. Photos are edited and delivered after each session for use on social media and the website.
  • BFacebook. Provider manages Client's Facebook Page: posting about times per week, keeping business info current, and posting inventory, offers and events Client approves.
  • CInstagram. Provider creates an Instagram account in Client's name, sets up the profile and branding, and posts on the same schedule as Facebook. Client owns the account.
  • DCustomer relationship management. Provider monitors and replies to messages and comments that arrive through Facebook, Instagram and other connected social channels, Monday to Saturday, within one hour during business hours. Provider then directs customers to the dealership owner, P.O. Vahabzadegan, through his Facebook or phone number, where he discusses specifics with them. All messages, leads and appointment requests are forwarded to him directly. Provider does not quote prices, financing or trade-in values, or make sales commitments, unless Client has supplied approved answers.
  • EWebsite inventory and SEO. Provider adds Client's current inventory, estimated at 35 or more vehicles, to Client's website, entering each one individually: year, make, model, trim, VIN, mileage, price, features, photos and a written description. Provider also writes SEO page titles, meta descriptions and image alt text for each listing. Each vehicle added after the initial upload costs $75, billed with the next monthly invoice.

4Client responsibilities

Client will give Provider admin or editor access to the Facebook Page, Meta Business Suite, Instagram account and website inventory tools within 3 days of signing. Client will supply accurate vehicle details, pricing and legal disclosures, give access to the lot and vehicles for photo sessions, review content within 2 business days, and report price or availability changes promptly. Client is responsible for the accuracy and legal compliance of its prices, offers and advertising claims. Provider posts what Client supplies and approves.

5Ownership and content

All social accounts and website listings belong to Client. Provider keeps the copyright in photos it takes and gives Client a permanent license to use them for its business. Provider may show the work in a portfolio unless Client objects in writing. Client confirms it has the right to any logos, text and images it provides.

6Payment

Payment is due on the 1st of each month by invoice or electronic payment. The first payment is due when this agreement is signed. If a payment is more than 5 days late, Provider may pause services until it is paid. Balances more than 10 days past due carry a 5% late fee.

7Ending early

Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice. Text or email counts as written notice. Client pays for all work through the end of the notice period, and fees for a month already started are not refunded. Once fees are paid, Provider hands over all account access, content and files.

8Results and liability

Provider will work professionally and in good faith but cannot guarantee sales, leads, followers, reach, search rankings or reviews, because these depend on the platforms and the market. Platform changes, suspensions or outages outside Provider's control are not a breach. Provider's total liability is limited to the fees paid for the month in which a claim arises, and neither party is liable for lost profits or indirect damages.

9Confidentiality and data use

Provider uses Client's logins, customer information, messages and business data only to perform these services. Provider will not sell, share or pass this information to other providers or third parties, except as needed to use the platforms Client's accounts run on. Provider will not use it to solicit Client's customers or business for anyone else, and will not use it for any malicious purpose, including unauthorized access, hacking, identity theft or any other way of stealing information. Provider keeps account credentials secure, uses them only for Client's accounts, and returns or deletes them when this agreement ends. Client likewise keeps Provider's business details confidential.

10General

Provider is an independent contractor and not an employee. Changes must be in writing. This is the full agreement between the parties. It is governed by Oklahoma law, and disputes are handled in Tulsa County. If any part is unenforceable, the rest stays in effect.

11Signatures

Both parties agreed to sign electronically. Drawn or typed signatures here have the same effect as handwritten ones.
